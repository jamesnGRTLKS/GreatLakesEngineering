Agile productions using alternative products

Replace products without stock with alternative ones


Demo video of Alternative MO Products available at Arxi YouTube Channel

  • This app offers an alternative solution for managing product substitutions in a product-based environment.

  • When executing a Manufacturing Order involving the primary product, this app ensures that the alternative product is consumed in the event of unavailability.

  • Configure your product variants with their alternative products.

  • Configure the ration of your alternative (ex: when using this alternative we need to use 2 quantities instead of 1)


Configuration

  • Navigate to Product Variants within the Sales or Inventory sections, to access the desired product that requires modification.

  • Look for the "Alternative Products" notebook specifically dedicated to this purpose

  • Click on it and add the substitute product to the lines below the page.

  • Once the aforementioned configuration is finalized, the module will automatically designate the alternative product whenever the quantities of the primary product fall short of the Bill of Materials requirements during a Manufacturing Order.
